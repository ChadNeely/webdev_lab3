Welcome to Chad Neely's Website

This website introduces my interests, professional goals, and a memorable weekend trip to Grand Island. The three pages are connected through the navigation list at the top of every page.

About This Site

I am building this website as part of ITMD-361 to practice writing clear, organized HTML from scratch. The pages use semantic HTML elements, descriptive headings, accessible alternative text, and directory-based links between the documents.

My goal is to create web pages that are easy to read and useful to visitors. A good website should communicate its subject clearly, provide dependable navigation, and make its content available to people using different devices and browsing tools.

What You Can Find Here

  • The home page explains the purpose of this small website.
  • The resume page presents my professional interests, skills, education, and experience.
  • The Grand Island page describes a weekend backpacking trip with my friend Luca.

Interest in Outdoor Adventure

Outdoor trips are a great way to slow down, explore unfamiliar places, and learn how to work with a travel partner. Planning transportation, food, equipment, and a daily route requires organization and flexibility. Those same habits are useful when working on a technical project.
